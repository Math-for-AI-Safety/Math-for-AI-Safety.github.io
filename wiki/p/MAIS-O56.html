Vanishing weight decay selects both S_3 representations

MAIS-O56 · open problem

Summary

How far a mechanistic interpretation of one trained network carries to another depends on whether training selects the same circuits each time. For networks trained on group multiplication, Chughtai, Chan, and Nanda [CCN23] found universality in the weak sense only: the trained circuits are built from irreducible characters of the group, but which irreducibles appear varies from one random seed to the next.
