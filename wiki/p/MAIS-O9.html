Steering Conway's Life from a small seed

MAIS-O9 · open problem

Summary

Can a small controller reliably steer a vast system to a desired global state, despite noise it cannot remove and rival processes it did not design? This is an abstraction of a core difficulty of AI alignment: goal-directed control of a complex world from limited leverage, with the difficulty concentrated where it is in practice — robustness and competition.
