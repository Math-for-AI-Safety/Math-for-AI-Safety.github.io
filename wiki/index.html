Math for AI Safety

MAIS is an open wiki of precisely stated mathematical problems whose solutions would advance AI safety. It has 92 open problems in 8 agendas. Each problem comes with a theory of change, is checked against the literature when it is released, and is reviewed in public.

How MAIS is different

Several efforts to automate alignment research are run privately. MAIS is open by default: problems, submissions, reviews and verdicts are all public, so anyone can read, challenge and build on the work as it happens.

  • Public problems. Each statement, its theory of change and its status live in a public repository.
  • Public review. Reviews happen in open issues and pull requests, with a record of what was checked and what was not.
  • Public credit and AI use. AI coauthors are welcome when disclosed; every claim is verified by a person, in the open.
